COMMON QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Have a different question? Send me a message and tell me what you're thinking.

What kind of chatbots can you build?＋

Business support bots, website assistants, personal AI assistants, document-based knowledge bots, creator/community assistants, and other scoped AI experiences.

Can the chatbot use my business information?＋

Yes, depending on the project. It can be designed to use approved FAQs, documents, product details, or other provided information. The setup and limitations will be agreed before development.

Can it be added to my website?＋

Website integration can be included when the chosen platform and technical setup support it. We'll confirm the integration requirements during planning.

How much does a chatbot cost?＋

Pricing depends on features, integrations, knowledge sources, and complexity. Share your idea and I'll help define a realistic scope and quote.

How long does a project take?＋

Timelines depend on scope and integrations. A focused prototype is usually simpler than a production system with multiple integrations, testing, and deployment requirements.
